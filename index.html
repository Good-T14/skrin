Drawing

  • Pick a tool on the left, then click the start and end point (or click-and-drag).
  • Click a drawing to select it, drag it to move, drag its dots to reshape.
  • Del deletes · Esc cancels · Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Magnet snaps to the open / high / low / close of the nearest bar.
  • Measure shows the price change, % and number of bars between two points.

Moving around

  • Mouse wheel zooms, drag pans, drag the price axis to stretch it. Buttons bottom-left jump to 1M, 3M ... All.

VWAP

  • Indicators ▾ → VWAP. It restarts every quarter, year, month or week (your choice).
  • Green lines are ±1σ and blue dashed lines ±2σ (volume-weighted standard deviations). Price beyond ±2σ is statistically stretched.
  • The index itself often has no volume on Yahoo; then all days are weighted equally.
